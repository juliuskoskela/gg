# View headers

Tabs represent multiple views sharing one content area. A lone view instead has a plain title, a restrained six-dot grip when docking is available, and a close action at the trailing edge. Stacked views use a tabs-only row; their active controls live in a separate toolbar below it. Single-view summaries and controls share the title row where space permits and wrap below it in panels at 520px or less.

The design preserves GG’s palette, IBM Plex typography, 12px panel inset, thin rules, and compact desktop controls. Narrow and touch targets remain 44px. The grip identifies the title as the docking handle; close actions and content tools are separate from drag listeners. Single bodies are named regions, and stacked bodies retain the tab/tabpanel relationship. Existing focus IDs and keyboard docking remain available.

Apple’s [tab-view guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/tab-views) defines tabs as mutually exclusive panes in the same area and keeps controls scoped to their pane. Its [toolbar guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/toolbars) distinguishes content actions from tab navigation, recommends logical control groups, concise titles, and restrained backgrounds. Its [window guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/windows) places movement in the frame above content. These principles inform the hierarchy and title grip; GG retains its own dockable-panel appearance.

Browser critique checked single, stacked, and split arrangements at 1440, 768, 390, and 320px in both themes. At compact widths the existing workspace presents open views as one stack; one remaining view still has a plain title. Review confirmed separate tab and toolbar rows, grouped Diff statistics, trailing controls, no header overflow, and visible active tabs. The narrow stacked strip scrolls horizontally to retain access to all view tabs.

Validation: Svelte and TypeScript checks; 25 targeted panel/docking unit tests; 24 live browser layout/theme cases with zero page errors; pointer grouping, keyboard tab selection, keyboard splitting, pointer/keyboard cancellation, retained focus, and close controls isolated from dragging. Live checks used a read-only shared backend and blocked mutation endpoints. Updated end-to-end contracts are included for the integrated test run.
